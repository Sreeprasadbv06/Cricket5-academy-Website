The Cricket5 Method

Five fundamentals. Every session.

Every drill on our schedule maps back to one of five pillars — so players and parents always know what a session is building toward.

01 — Technique

Batting & Bowling Basics

Grip, stance, seam position and release, corrected early so good habits outlast a growth spurt.

02 — Fitness

Movement & Conditioning

Speed between the wickets, throwing power and injury-resistant strength, built age-appropriately.

03 — Game Sense

Reading the Match

Field placements, shot selection and bowling changes — taught through small-sided match play, not just lectures.

04 — Temperament

Composure Under Pressure

Simulated last-over and new-ball scenarios so nerves are familiar long before a real final.

05 — Teamwork

Fielding as a Unit

Backing up, calling and captaincy basics — the parts of the game that don't show up in a scorecard.
