Junior Starters

A first, unintimidating introduction to cricket. Coaches focus on hand-eye coordination, throwing and catching games, and a modified soft-ball format so every child bats and bowls in every session.

  • Soft-ball equipment provided
  • Fun-first drills, mini matches every week
  • Progress note sent home each term
